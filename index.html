What this is

We installed GeoZilla, walked to the paywall, and held it up against the report.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, and State of Paywalls 2026 is what we learned from 78,000+ of their paywalls. We went through GeoZilla the way a new user does: tracking prompt, welcome, the role picker, then the paywall. We also read your App Store listing. Then we compared what your paywall does with what the report says.

The report is App Store data, and most of GeoZilla's revenue comes from Google Play, India first. So we compare how your paywall is set up, not your conversion or your prices. Where the report splits a number by category, we use Lifestyle, your App Store category.

What follows is five ideas, each one built into a clickable prototype. The prototype uses your design, not ours.

We did not redesign your app

Every pixel in the prototype is yours: the map, the dark sheet, the scrolling feature chips, the three plan cards, the teal Continue button. We changed what is pre-selected, what is written on it, and what happens on close.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If you have already run one of these, tell us and we will go deeper on the rest.
